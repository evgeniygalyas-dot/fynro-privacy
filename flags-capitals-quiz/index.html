About this policy

This policy describes how the current version of Flags & Capitals Quiz, published by FYNRO STUDIOS LTD, handles data. It reflects the verified audit findings for the current game.

Data stored locally

The game stores progress, stars, records, attempts and settings locally on your device. These records support gameplay and remember your preferences. The current game has no account system, analytics, advertising or purchases.

Device identifiers

Godot, the engine used by the game, may access the Android ID locally on your device. The audit did not identify transmission of this identifier. Local access to an identifier is distinct from sending it to an external service.

Network use and sharing

The game operates offline. No app-controlled network transmission was identified in the current game, and no data is knowingly shared by the app with external services.

This policy describes the app's behavior. Android, Google and other platform services may handle data independently under their own settings and policies; this policy does not describe or make assurances about their independent data collection.

Resetting your data

You can reset local game progress using Reset Progress in the game. You can also clear the app's locally stored data through Android's app storage settings. Android's menu names may vary by device and version.

Future versions and advertising

Future versions may introduce ads. Before releasing a version with ads, we will update this Privacy Policy and the relevant Google Play declarations to reflect the new behavior and any data handling it introduces.

Policy changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Please review this page when updating the app to learn about changes to its data handling.
